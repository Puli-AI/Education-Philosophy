
# Cognitive Skill Integration: 3Cs in AI Thinking

## 1. Complex Problem Solving + Critical Thinking = Analytical Reasoning (分析推理能力)

**Justification:**  
Combining complex problem solving with critical thinking yields **analytical reasoning**, which involves identifying patterns, logically breaking down complex scenarios, critically evaluating evidence, and systematically verifying solutions.

- **Academic Support:**  
  According to Funke (2010), complex problem solving inherently requires systematic analysis, which becomes more powerful and accurate when coupled with critical thinking, emphasizing evidence evaluation and logical consistency.  
  *(Funke, J. (2010). Complex problem solving: A case for complex cognition? Cognitive Processing, 11(2), 133–142.)*

---

## 2. Complex Problem Solving + Creativity = Innovative Problem Formulation (创新问题构建能力)

**Justification:**  
When complex problem solving intersects with creativity, the outcome is **innovative problem formulation**, a skill crucial for redefining, reframing, and approaching problems from unconventional angles, thus enabling original solutions.

- **Academic Support:**  
  Research by Mumford et al. (2012) argues that creativity enhances problem-solving by enabling individuals to frame and reframe problems innovatively, allowing more original and effective solutions to emerge.  
  *(Mumford, M. D., Medeiros, K. E., & Partlow, P. J. (2012). Creative thinking: Processes, strategies, and knowledge. The Journal of Creative Behavior, 46(1), 30–47.)*

---

## 3. Critical Thinking + Creativity = Evaluative Creativity (评价性创造力)

**Justification:**  
The fusion of critical thinking and creativity forms **evaluative creativity**, characterized by the ability to generate novel ideas while simultaneously assessing their viability, originality, and practicality.

- **Academic Support:**  
  Paul & Elder (2006) emphasize that genuine creativity involves critical judgment to distinguish truly innovative ideas from merely novel ones, promoting creative solutions that are not only new but also relevant.  
  *(Paul, R., & Elder, L. (2006). Critical Thinking: Tools for Taking Charge of Your Learning and Your Life. Pearson/Prentice Hall.)*

---

## 4. Complex Problem Solving + Critical Thinking + Creativity = Integrated Cognitive Excellence (综合认知卓越能力)

**Justification:**  
When all three cognitive skills converge—complex problem solving, critical thinking, and creativity—the result is **integrated cognitive excellence**, a holistic cognitive state facilitating innovative yet logical solutions to intricate, multi-dimensional problems that require balanced judgment, originality, and rigorous analysis.

- **Academic Support:**  
  Sternberg’s theory of successful intelligence (2003) highlights that the integration of analytical, creative, and practical intelligence results in superior problem-solving performance.  
  *(Sternberg, R. J. (2003). Wisdom, intelligence, and creativity synthesized. Cambridge University Press.)*
