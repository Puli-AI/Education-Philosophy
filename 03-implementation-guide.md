# Implementation Guide

Practical strategies for applying the integrated framework in your learning or problem‑solving journey.

## Getting Started

1. **Identify Your Challenge**  
   Start with curiosity—what problem needs solving, or which concept needs deeper understanding?

2. **Map Your Knowledge**  
   Use AI to explore the topic and identify what you already know vs. what you need to learn.

3. **Apply the Framework**  
   Iterate through the 5C components while following Feynman’s steps.

## AI Integration Tips

- **Effective Prompting** – Be specific about your learning goals and current understanding.
- **Iterative Refinement** – Use AI feedback to continuously improve explanations and comprehension.
- **Critical Evaluation** – Always critically assess AI outputs and verify important information.

## Example Application: Climate Change Solutions

- **Curiosity** – Explore promising climate change solutions  
- **Complex Problem Solving** – Break down renewable energy systems  
- **Creativity** – Generate novel carbon capture ideas  
- **Critical Thinking** – Evaluate feasibility of proposed solutions  
- **Communication** – Explain your approach to stakeholders
