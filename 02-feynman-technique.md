# The Feynman Technique

Master any subject through the power of simple explanation and iterative refinement:

1. **Select a concept and map your knowledge**  
   Write down everything you know about a topic. Create a visual map to represent your understanding.

2. **Teach it to a 12‑year‑old**  
   Explain the concept in simple terms. If you can’t, you don’t truly understand it yet.

3. **Review and Refine**  
   Identify gaps in your explanation and return to the source material to fill them.

4. **Test and Archive**  
   Test your understanding by teaching someone else. Then archive your refined explanation for future review.
